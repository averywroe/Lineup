How to play

Each day brings six bars, one of each kind of puzzle:

  • Note: name the note on the staff.
  • Key: name the key from its key signature.
  • Interval: name the distance between two notes.
  • Chord: name the chord, even when it's inverted.
  • Build: play the chord on the keyboard. Any octave, any order.
  • Ear: listen, then name what you heard.

You get two tries per bar. First try earns 2 points, second try 1. A perfect day is 12, a perfect authentic cadence.

The week gets harder like a crossword: Monday and Tuesday are easy, Wednesday to Friday are medium, and the weekend is hard. A new puzzle arrives at midnight.

Practice mode gives you unlimited sets at any level, or drills one puzzle type.
